Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy covers the The Answer Isss… quiz channels on YouTube and Facebook, this website, and The Answer Isss Uploader, a small tool we use to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel.

Viewers of our videos

We do not collect personal information from people who watch our videos. Views, comments and likes happen on YouTube and Facebook and are handled under their privacy policies (Google Privacy Policy, Meta Privacy Policy). We only see the totals and summaries those platforms show every channel owner.

Children

Our videos are made to be enjoyed by curious kids together with their families. We never ask children for personal information, we do not run contests or sign-ups, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

This website

This website has no sign-up, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The Answer Isss Uploader (Google API use)

The Uploader is used only by the channel owner to publish videos to the The Answer Isss… YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube API Services. When the channel owner signs in with Google, the Uploader receives permission to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and captions to that channel (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl);
  • add those videos to the channel's playlists.

The Uploader does not read, store or share any other Google user data. The sign-in token is kept only on the owner's own computer and is never sent to any server other than Google's. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

The Answer Isss Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

AI voices

The voices in our videos are made with AI text-to-speech (ElevenLabs). We label this on every video.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. To reach us, send a message to our Facebook Page or through our YouTube channel.
